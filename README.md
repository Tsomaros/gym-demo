# Gym app: demo

**▶ [Open the demo](https://tsomaros.github.io/gym-demo/)**

## What is this?

An app that a gym uses to run its day: members, memberships and payments, entry with a QR code, group classes and training plans.

Here you can **try it with a made-up gym** and see how it works. There is nothing to install, and you need no account or password.

## How to start

1. Open the [demo](https://tsomaros.github.io/gym-demo/).
2. The app starts in Greek. For English, press "English" at the top of the page.
3. Pick a role.
4. Look around using the menu. "Change role", at the top of the screen, shows you the app from another side.

## Which role should I pick?

| Role | Who that is | What they see |
|---|---|---|
| **Member** | Someone who trains there | Their entry code, membership, classes, training plan and progress |
| **Reception** | The person at the front desk | Who is coming in, the members, membership sales and renewals |
| **Trainer** | Someone who coaches the members | Classes, their clients and those clients' plans |
| **Owner** | The person who runs the gym | An overview with income, payments, staff and the history of changes |

## Things to try

- **Walk into the gym.** As a member, press "Simulate a scan at the door". Then change role to reception and see the visit.
- **Sell a membership.** As reception, open "Members", pick a member and press "New membership". Then see the payment as the owner, under "Payments".
- **Book a class.** As a member, open "Classes", choose a day and press "Book".

## Good to know

- **Everything is made up:** the names, payments and visits.
- **What you do stays in your browser.** It is not sent anywhere and nobody else sees it.
- **Want a fresh start?** Press "Reset". The demo also starts over when you open it on another day, so that its dates match today.
- **Not everything works.** Uploading photos, emails and notifications, for example, need the real app. You will then see "Not available in the demo".
